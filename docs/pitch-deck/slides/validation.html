Demo scenario: six candidates, three rejected on attempt 2 of 3

09

This is the most important design decision. The model suggests plans; deterministic Python decides whether they are allowed. Of six candidates, the cheapest is a dry van, one doesn’t have the capacity, one driver is out of hours — all rejected with a reason the UI shows. Learned preferences can only re-rank plans that already pass. Nothing an agent says, and nothing in a driver’s message, can turn off a hard check.
