The problem

Cold-chain failures don’t wait for office hours

Mendocino County, CA · Jan 2021

A freezer failed around 2 a.m. 830 vaccine doses had about two hours left. Staff scrambled by phone to find people to vaccinate.

Maine · Jan 2021

4,400 doses bound for 35 sites went out of range in transit. Sensors flagged it; people re-planned and reshipped by hand.

$35B

Up to 50%

Sources: NPR via KISU (5 Jan 2021); Boston Globe (20 Jan 2021); IQVIA Institute 2019 cold-chain survey (est.); WHO via UNEP

These are real incidents. In Mendocino County a hospital freezer failed around 2 a.m. and 830 doses had roughly two hours. In Maine, 4,400 Moderna doses went out of range in transit; sensors caught it, but the response — re-planning, reshipping — was manual. Industry-wide, IQVIA estimates about 35 billion dollars a year lost to temperature-controlled logistics failures, and the WHO estimates up to half of vaccines are wasted, with the cold chain a main cause. Be honest: the Mendocino case was a storage freezer, not a truck.
