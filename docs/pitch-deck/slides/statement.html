Today: one dispatcher, 30–50 drivers, a phone, and the middle of the night.

Ghost Dispatcher rescues the load before anyone wakes up.

Today this is a human job: a dispatcher at a large carrier can be looking after 30 to 50 drivers, and the response happens by phone, at night, under a hard spoilage deadline. Ghost Dispatcher turns that into an automated loop — and the important word is “self-healing”: it keeps going even when the systems it depends on fail.
